Privacy Policy

TNAU GPA & OGPA Engine, developed by Prathish P. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

This website calculates GPA and OGPA from the marks you type in. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What we do not do

We do not run a server or database. We do not collect, store, sell or share your marks, name, ID number or email address. The developer cannot see your data.

Where your marks are kept

  • If you use the site as a guest: your entries are saved only in your own browser, on your own device.
  • If you sign in with Google: your entries are saved in a hidden application folder in your own Google Drive. Only this website, when you are signed in, can open that folder. Other files in your Drive are not accessible to it.

What Google permission is requested

  • Google Drive application data folder (drive.appdata): to save and load your marks file in that hidden folder.
  • Your email address and basic profile: only to show which Google account is signed in. It is not stored on any server of ours.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services the page loads

To work, the page loads code, icons and fonts from public services (Google Sign-In, Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS and Lucide icons). Like any website, these services can see your IP address and browser type when the page loads. Your marks are not sent to them.

Your control

  • Use the delete button on a profile to remove it.
  • You can delete the hidden app data at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps → this app → Delete all connections or Manage app data, or remove the access entirely.
  • Clearing your browser data removes the guest copy.

Children

The site is intended for university students and collects no personal information from anyone.
